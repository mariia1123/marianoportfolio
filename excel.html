TikTok & Financial Performance Analysis

2024

Interactive dashboard investigating whether social media presence on TikTok correlates with improved financial performance. Analyzed 10 food & beverage companies (2018–2022) using Tobin's Q valuation metrics and Return on Sales (ROS) efficiency measures. Built linear regression models to evaluate the relationship between TikTok adoption and firm performance, with deep-dive case study on Oatly Group AB.

Sowiso Performance Analysis

2023

Comparative analysis of first-week math homework performance across 81 Maastricht BA students. Aggregated and visualized Sowiso platform data (score vs duration) across 64 mathematical sub-themes, identifying outliers and revealing why raw averages can mislead.

Weather Data & Wine Quality

2020

Multiple linear regression model predicting Bordeaux daily temperature using neighboring weather stations (Nantes, Limoges, Toulouse, Pamplona). Trained on 3 years of ECAD climate data and validated on holdout 2020-21 dataset. Achieved R² = 0.97 for powdery mildew prevention application.
