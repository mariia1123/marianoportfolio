Case II — Financial Metrics Analysis

Group project · RStudio · Compustat database · May 2023

Group of 4Data cleaningFinancial ratios2007-2008 crisis

Goal

Analyze financial metrics of publicly listed firms from the Compustat database during the 2007–2008 financial crisis period. The project involved cleaning raw financial data, computing valuation multiples, profitability ratios, and efficiency metrics, then identifying outliers and summarizing the distributions of key financial indicators.

Dataset & Setup

We started with 142,441 firm-year observations from the Compustat database. After filtering for active firms (costat == "A") with complete financial data across fiscal years 2007 and 2008, we retained 6,353 observations covering balance sheet, income statement, and market price data.

Data Cleaning Pipeline

  • Missing value handling: Filtered rows with missing values in critical fields (net income, total assets, common shares outstanding) to ensure complete records
  • Validation constraints: Removed firms with zero or negative assets and shares outstanding to avoid division errors
  • Secondary price sources: Used alternative price fields (prcc_f) as fallbacks where primary prices (prcc_c) were unavailable
  • Outlier removal: Applied Z-score filtering (threshold: 3 standard deviations) across computed ratios, reducing dataset to 6,283 valid observations

All metrics were carefully constructed to handle edge cases: earnings per share was set to 0.01 (rather than zero) to prevent division errors in P/E calculations, and we substituted missing equity values with (total assets – long-term debt) where available.

Descriptive Statistics

The final cleaned dataset revealed striking patterns during the crisis year:

  • Market-to-Book (MTB): Median 1.49, but highly right-skewed (mean 5.55) — reflecting a small cohort of overvalued firms
  • P/E Ratio: Median 10.2, but some firms with negative earnings pushed the distribution left (mean 9.54)
  • ROA & ROE: Both exhibited negative mean returns in 2008, confirming the severity of the financial crisis impact on profitability
The 2007–2008 period captures the pre-crisis (2007) and acute crisis (2008) phases, showing sharp deterioration in profitability and valuation multiples year-over-year.

Variable Engineering

We created derived metrics to capture firm dynamics:

  • Firm size (size): Natural log of total assets, used as a control variable in later models
  • Investment intensity (inv): Year-over-year change in log assets, indicating growth or contraction
  • Book value per share: Equity divided by common shares outstanding, grounding P/B and MTB calculations

My Role

Collaborated with three team members (Youri, led the initial data setup). I focused on data transformation logic, constructing the metric formulas, implementing outlier detection, and validating the cleaned dataset before passing it to the team for cross-sectional analysis.

Deliverables

  • Cleaned and documented dataset (6,283 firm-years)
  • Comprehensive summary statistics table for MTB, P/E, ROA, ROE
  • Data quality report with missing value inventory and outlier count
  • R script documenting all filtering and transformation steps
