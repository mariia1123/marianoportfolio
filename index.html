BIO

I've spent the last five years turning spreadsheets into decisions, mostly for product and growth teams.

My favorite work sits where rigor meets storytelling — a number is only useful when someone acts on it. I care a lot about how analysis is communicated: the difference between a chart that informs and one that just decorates.

Currently based in Madrid, working remotely with teams across Europe and the US.

WHAT I DO

Analytics & reporting — Building dashboards and metrics frameworks that teams actually use, not the kind that get bookmarked and forgotten.

Experimentation — Designing and analyzing A/B tests, with proper statistical rigor and (importantly) clear writeups.

Forecasting & modeling — Predictive models for churn, demand, and revenue — built to be understood by non-technical stakeholders.

Data storytelling — Turning analyses into narratives. Sometimes that's a deck, sometimes a memo, sometimes an interactive piece.
